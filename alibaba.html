Credits & Model Access
Alibaba Cloud Model Studio provides trial credits for new users. Sign in at modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com to check your balance, manage API keys, and access Qwen models (Qwen3, Qwen-Max, Qwen-Plus, Qwen-Turbo). The Model Experience Center lets you test models interactively before deploying.
